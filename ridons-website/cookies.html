Cookie Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

This website doesn't use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, so there is nothing to accept or reject.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar tools include local storage and tracking pixels.

This website

  • No tracking or analytics cookies. We don't measure your visit or follow you to other sites.
  • No advertising cookies or pixels.
  • No forms. We don't collect your name, number or email on this website.

Services that load on this page

  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address to deliver them. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider keeps short-term server logs, such as IP address and time of visit, to keep the site secure and working.

The Ridons app

The app stores a few things on your phone so it works, for example to keep you signed in and remember your language. These are needed for the app to run and are not used for advertising.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.
