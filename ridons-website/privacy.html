Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run safe, fair rides and pay captains.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • This website has no forms, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Ridons runs a moto ride app in Kigali that connects passengers with independent captains. We are responsible for your personal data under Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data and privacy (Law N° 058/2021 of 13/10/2021).

What we collect

If you ride as a passenger

  • Your name and phone number, confirmed by an SMS code.
  • Pickup and drop-off points, and your location during a trip.
  • The offers you make, the price you agree and how you paid. For MoMo payments we receive a transaction reference. We never see your PIN.
  • Ratings you give and receive, and messages you send to support.

If you are a captain

  • Everything above, plus your National ID, motorcycle registration, insurance, photo and plate number.
  • Your MoMo number, trips and earnings history.
  • Your training attendance and the ID number on your Ridons vest.

On this website

Nothing that identifies you. The website has no sign-up forms, no analytics and no advertising tools. To show the page, our hosting provider and Google Fonts receive your IP address and basic browser details, as any website does. See our Cookie Policy.

Why we use it

  • To provide rides: matching you with captains, sharing pickup details and processing payment.
  • To keep people safe: verifying captains, handling reports and investigating disputes using the trip record.
  • To pay captains and keep a record of their earnings that they can see in the app.
  • To meet the law: for example tax records and lawful requests from authorities and regulators.
  • With your consent only: news and offers by SMS or email. You can say no, or stop them at any time.

A captain's income record

Each captain's trips and earnings build a record that belongs to them. We share it with a bank, savings scheme or insurer only when the captain asks us to, and only the parts needed.

Who we share it with

  • Your captain or passenger: only what is needed for the trip, such as first name, photo, rating, plate number and pickup point.
  • Payment providers such as MTN MoMo, to process payments.
  • Service providers that host our systems or send SMS for us, under contracts that protect your data.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

We store data in Rwanda, or outside Rwanda only where the law allows it and with any authorisation it requires. Data is encrypted, ID numbers are encrypted separately, and only staff who need it can access it.

How long we keep it

We keep trip and payment records for as long as we need them to handle disputes and for as long as the law requires for financial records. Captain documents are kept while the account is active and for a limited time after it closes. After that we delete or anonymise the data.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you, or give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or withdraw consent you gave.

Email contact@ridons.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA), which supervises data protection in Rwanda.

Age

You must be 18 or older to create a Ridons account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
