The short version

  • Ridons connects passengers with independent moto captains.
  • The price you agree in the app is the price you pay. It can't change at drop-off.
  • Be respectful, wear a helmet and follow traffic law.

About these terms

These terms apply when you use the Ridons app or website. By creating an account you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use Ridons.

What Ridons does

Ridons is a technology platform. It lets passengers make offers for moto trips and lets independent captains accept, counter or skip them. Captains are not Ridons employees. Each captain provides the ride using their own motorcycle.

Your account

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Give accurate details and keep your phone number up to date.
  • Keep your account to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on it.

Agreeing a price

  • The app suggests a usual price for your route. You can send it or change it.
  • Captains can accept your offer or make a counter-offer. You choose which captain to ride with.
  • Once you both agree, the price is locked. Nobody can change it during or after the trip.
  • Prices have a minimum and a maximum, shown in the app, to keep them fair for both sides.

Paying

  • Passengers pay the agreed price by cash or MTN MoMo at the end of the trip, and nothing more.
  • If a MoMo payment fails, the trip stays open until it is paid, and you can't book another trip until then.
  • Captains pay Ridons a service fee on completed trips. The fee is shown in the captain app.

For refunds, see our Refund Policy.

Cancelling

If your plans change, cancel in the app as early as you can. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows are recorded and may affect your account.

Safety and conduct

  • Captains and passengers must wear a helmet and follow Rwanda's traffic laws.
  • Treat each other with respect. Harassment, threats, discrimination and violence are not allowed.
  • Captains must not ask for more than the agreed price.
  • Report any problem in the app or on WhatsApp. We review reports using the trip record.

For captains

  • Keep a valid National ID, driving licence, motorcycle registration and insurance.
  • Complete Ridons training before your first trip and wear your Ridons vest on every trip.
  • You are free to accept, counter or skip any request.

Lost items

Tell us in the app or on WhatsApp. We will contact the captain and help arrange the return. A small return fee may apply, and we will tell you before you agree to it.
