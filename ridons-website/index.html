The Ridons fare promise

Four rules that protect both seats.

A fair price for passengers and a fair living for motari, in writing, on every trip.

Agreed means final

The fare can't change at the drop-off. Disputes are checked against the negotiation record and the GPS route, and decided within 24 hours.

A floor and a ceiling

Fares start at RWF 500, so motari are never pushed too low. Offers are capped at 3× the usual high fare for the route, so passengers are never gouged.

One fee, shown up front

Ridons takes 8% of the agreed fare, only on completed trips. Passengers pay the agreed fare and nothing on top. There's no surge pricing.

Paid in seconds

The passenger pays by MTN MoMo at the end of the trip, and the motari's 92% arrives within 30 seconds. Airtel Money is coming next.

Help

Straight answers.

Can't find your question? Message us on WhatsApp. We reply within 30 minutes during business hours.

PassengersDo I have to negotiate every time?

No. Ridons shows the usual fare for your route, and you can send it as your offer in one tap. Negotiate only when you want to.

PassengersWhat if the motari asks for more at the end?

The agreed fare is final, and you pay it through the app. If someone asks for more, report it in the app or on WhatsApp. We check the negotiation record and decide within 24 hours.

PassengersHow do I pay?

With MTN MoMo. At the end of the trip you get a prompt on your phone. Approve it with your PIN and you get an SMS receipt. Airtel Money is coming next.

PassengersCan I choose my motari?

Yes. You see every offer with the motari's name, photo, rating, plate and arrival time, and you pick. Ridons doesn't assign you anyone.

MotariHow much of the fare do I keep?

92%. Ridons takes 8% of the agreed fare, and only on completed trips. On a RWF 2,000 trip you keep RWF 1,840.

MotariWhen do I get paid?

Within 30 seconds of the passenger paying, straight to your MTN MoMo wallet, with an SMS for every payment. If a passenger's payment fails, the trip stays open and they can't book again until they pay.

MotariCan I refuse a trip or set my own price?

Yes, always. For every request you can Accept, Counter with your own price, or Skip. Nobody sets your price for you.

MotariHow do I join, and what does it cost?
  1. Download the app and register with your phone number.
  2. Upload your National ID (both sides), motorcycle registration, current insurance and a photo of yourself.
  3. We review them within 24 hours and message you on WhatsApp.
  4. Attend a 2-hour training in Kinyarwanda: Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday at 10:00.
  5. Collect your vest and badge, go online and start taking trips.
EveryoneWhere does Ridons work?

In Kigali, starting with Kimihurura, Remera and nearby Gasabo neighbourhoods. We add new areas one at a time.

EveryoneIs Ridons allowed by RURA?

We work with the regulator. The negotiated-fare model has been presented to RURA, we share trip data with them every quarter, and we tell them before any change to pricing or rider checks.

EveryoneWhat happens to my data?

It's stored on Rwanda-compliant hosting and encrypted, and ID numbers are encrypted separately. We follow Rwanda's data protection law (Law N° 058/2021) and never sell your personal data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
