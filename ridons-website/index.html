FAQs

Common questions

Do I have to bargain every time?
Nope. We suggest a fair price for your trip. Tap Confirm and you're set. Only bargain if you feel like it.
Can I still pay with cash?
Yep. Cash or MTN MoMo, your choice. You pay exactly what you agreed. Not a franc more.
Where does Ridons work?
Kigali, starting with Kimihurura, Remera and Nyarutarama. More neighbourhoods are coming soon.
Is it faster than walking to the stage?
Way faster. Your rider comes to you, so there's no walking and no standing around bargaining.
How much of the fare does the rider keep?
Most of it. Ridons takes a small fee, only when a trip is finished. The rest goes straight to the rider.
What if the rider asks for more at the end?
Don't pay it. The agreed price is final. Report it in the app or on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out fast.
Can I choose my rider?
Always. You see every offer with name, photo, rating and plate, then you pick. We never pick for you.
I'm a rider. Can I skip a trip?
Anytime. Accept, counter or skip. Your bike, your price, your call.
I left something behind. Now what?
Hit us up in the app or on WhatsApp. We'll reach your rider and help get it back to you.
Is my data safe?
Yes. It's encrypted, protected under Rwanda's data protection law, and we never sell it.
