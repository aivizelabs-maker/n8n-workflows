Common questions

Do I have to bargain every time?
No. Tap Confirm to use the suggested price.
Can I pay with cash?
Yes. Cash or MTN MoMo. You pay what you agreed.
Where does Ridons work?
Live in Kigali: Kimihurura, Remera and Nyarutarama, with more areas coming.
Is it faster than walking to the stage?
Often. Your motari comes to you.
How much does the motari keep?
Most of it. Ridons takes a small fee on finished trips.
What if the motari asks for more?
Don't pay it. The agreed price is final. Report it in the app.
Can I choose my motari?
Yes. You see every offer and pick one.
I'm a motari. Can I skip a trip?
Anytime. Accept, counter or skip.
I left something behind.
Tell us in the app or email us. We'll contact your motari.
Is my data safe?
We keep only what we need and never sell it. See our Privacy Policy.
