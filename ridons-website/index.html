FAQs

Common questions

Do I have to negotiate every time?
No. We show you the usual price for your trip. Tap Confirm to send it as your offer. You only negotiate if you want to.
Can I still pay with cash?
Yes. You can pay with cash or MTN MoMo. Either way, you pay the price you agreed in the app, and not a franc more.
Where does Ridons operate?
We're live in Kigali, starting in busy areas like Kimihurura, Remera and Nyarutarama. We're adding new neighbourhoods one at a time.
Is it faster than walking to the stage?
Usually, yes. Our average wait in live areas is about 90 seconds, and the rider comes to you. You don't have to walk anywhere or stand around bargaining.
How much of my fare does the rider keep?
92%. Ridons takes 8%, and only when a trip is finished. On a RWF 2,000 trip, the rider keeps RWF 1,840.
What if the rider asks for more at the end?
The price you agreed is final. If someone asks for more, report it in the app or on WhatsApp. We check the trip record and decide within 24 hours.
Can I choose my rider?
Yes. You see every offer with the rider's name, photo, rating, plate number and price, and you pick. We never assign someone for you.
As a rider, can I turn down a trip?
Always. For every request you can accept, make a counter-offer, or skip it. Nobody sets your price for you.
What if I left something behind?
Tell us in the app or on WhatsApp. We contact the rider for you and try to get it back to you the same day. A small RWF 500 return fee may apply.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Your data is encrypted and stored in line with Rwanda's data protection law (Law N° 058/2021). We never sell your personal data.
